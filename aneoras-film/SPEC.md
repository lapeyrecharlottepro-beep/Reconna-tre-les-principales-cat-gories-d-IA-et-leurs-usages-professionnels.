# ANEORAS — « Votre potentiel. Notre stratégie. » — SPEC

GOAL:    The viewer (B2B decision-makers in private companies and in education) leaves feeling that ANEORAS reveals what an organisation already holds and turns it into the ability to act.
OUTPUT:  HyperFrames, 1920 x 1080, 30 fps
TIME:    94 s x 30 = 2820 frames. No music bed yet (see REVIEW.md); the script is the clock (timed for a French voiceover at ~2.4 words/s).

CONCEPT: "Le fil d'or". The brand signature (the gold curve and its four-point star) carries the whole film. It lights up a single window in a façade
         (the hidden potential), becomes a lever, a horizon line, a path, a strand, a learning road and a beam of perspective, then finally lands in the ANEORAS mark.
         Structure: Discovery → exploration → revelation (hidden potential → method → two audiences → conviction → signature).

LOOK:    Brand charter (guide supplied by the client):
         navy #05254E (trust, strategy) · red #B71932 (ambition, action) · gold #D6A13B, gradient #B88626 → #F4D58A → #D6A13B (value, the star)
         white #FFFFFF · light grey #E8E8E8 (structure lines) · dark grey #2B2B2B (secondary text)
         Grounds alternate by world: navy (façade, levers, education), white (lever, method, companies, technology, signature), red (ambition, ability to act).
         Montserrat: Bold 700 titles (64–110 px), SemiBold 600 sub-titles (40–60 px), Medium 500 labels (30–44 px), Light 300 wordmark.
         Flat graphic medium: solid colour planes, 4–16 px lines, architectural geometry. Accent colour on one word per shot.
OBJECTS: building façade (window grid), four-point star, lever and fulcrum, gold horizon line, magnifying ring, parallel paths, stacked blocks,
         route with stops, three strands (counsel, training, technology), process cards, learning path with milestones, human figure and its projected shadow,
         doors opening onto a perspective, three gold bars "Ξ" turning into an arrow, the ANEORAS mark.
MOTION:  enter expo.out / power4.out 0.6–0.9 s; camera power2.inOut; exits power3.in, 40–50 % shorter; titles per word blur 6→0 px, 0.07 s stagger, then dead still.
SHOTS:   see STORYBOARD.md (11 scenes, each one opening on the object the previous scene left).
BANNED:  dark void with small floating objects, glow/bloom/particles, lens flares, purple/blue gradients, glass cards, three cards in a row,
         everything fading-and-sliding up, text moving while read, logo docked in a corner, breathing/floating loops.
QA:      t=0.5 (façade + title), 6.5 (zoom through window), 9.5 (lever pressed), 14 (ambition), 19/23/25 (understand/accompany/build),
         30/33/36 (route), 46 (staircase), 52/56 (processes), 68 (path), 74/80 (shadow / doors), 85 (Ξ → arrow), 92 (signature).
