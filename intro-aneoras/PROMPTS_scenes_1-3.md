# ANEORAS – The Journey to the Stars
## Prompts for generating scenes 1 to 3 (photorealistic AI video)

Tools: Google Veo 3, OpenAI Sora, Runway Gen-4, Kling 2.x or HeyGen HyperFrames.
Settings: 16:9, 4K (or 1080p then upscale), 24 fps, **no text, no logo, no subtitles**.

**Tip:** generate each shot separately, then use the **last frame of each shot as the first frame** of the next one (image-to-video). That gives continuous camera movement.

**Common style suffix** (append to every prompt):
> Photorealistic premium cinematic commercial, anamorphic lens, shallow depth of field, soft volumetric light, deep midnight blue, black, white and champagne gold color palette, smooth stabilized camera movement, elegant, magical yet realistic, film grain, no text, no logo, no people.

---

### Shot 1 – Through the window (0–4 s)
> Interior of an elegant contemporary architect house at dusk, minimalist living room with warm indirect lighting, natural stone and light oak, a large floor-to-ceiling panoramic glass window. Slow dolly-in towards the window. Outside, a calm landscape under a deep blue evening sky; a single beautiful bright golden star shines alone in the sky, perfectly centered in the window. Peaceful, contemplative atmosphere.

### Shot 2 – Rising above the world (4–8 s)
> Seamless continuation: the camera glides through the open panoramic window and rises smoothly into the evening sky. The contemporary house becomes smaller below, its warm windows glowing; the camera ascends above rooftops, trees and a softly lit landscape. Majestic, fluid drone-like ascent, as if taking flight. The single golden star stays centered in the upper part of the frame.

### Shot 3 – The ascension (8–14 s)
> Continuous vertical ascent through soft clouds lit by the last light of dusk, then beyond the Earth's atmosphere. The sky gradually shifts from twilight blue to deep midnight blue, then to the black of outer space. Thousands of stars appear around the camera. One golden star shines brighter than all the others; the camera moves forward towards it, the star growing slowly with a subtle elegant lens flare. End on the golden star centered in a black starry sky.

### Shot 4 – The ANEORAS star (14–20 s)
**Already produced:** `ANEORAS_star_reveal_4K.mp4` (7 s, 3840×2160, 24 fps).
It opens on a starry sky with the golden star at the center. Join it to the end of shot 3 with a cross-dissolve of about 0.5 s. That gives roughly 13 s + 7 s = 20 s.

The final logo appears on the charter navy blue background (#05254E).

Do not have the AI generate the logo: video generators distort typography. The reveal uses your original logo, faithfully.

### Assembly (DaVinci Resolve, Premiere, CapCut…)
1. Shots 1 → 2 → 3, with short cross-fades (8–12 frames) if needed.
2. `ANEORAS_star_reveal_4K.mp4` at the end, dissolved on the golden star.
3. Music: a cinematic orchestral swell or ambient piano, climaxing on the golden light at around 16 s.
