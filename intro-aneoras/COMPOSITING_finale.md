# ANEORAS – Final sequence: compositing the logo

**File:** `ANEORAS_finale_4K.mp4` – 3840×2160, 24 fps, 20 s. The final frame stays still from 17.2 s to 20 s.

## Where to place the logo
Use the logo version with a transparent background: `logo_rgba.png` (3162×646 px).

| Parameter | Value (4K frame 3840×2160) |
|---|---|
| Logo width | **1920 px** (50% of the frame width) |
| Logo height | 392 px (proportional) |
| Top-left corner | **x = 960, y = 884** (centered) |
| Logo star position | x ≈ 1314.6, y ≈ 957.5 |

With this placement, the star in the video lands exactly on the logo's star (same position, same size, same proportions). The logo therefore seems to "grow" out of the star.

In 1080p, divide all values by 2: width 960 px, corner at (480, 442).

## Suggested animation in post-production
1. 16.5–17.2 s: the video's star settles into place.
2. 17.2 s: fade in the A and the wordmark over about 0.8 s (the star is already in place).
3. Hold on the full logo until the end.
